Safety Management System (SMS) - Cargo Operations Manual
Version: 1.2
Date: 2026-01-01

Section 1: General Cargo Safety
All crew members must follow safety precautions during cargo loading and discharging. Safe working load (SWL) of cargo gear must not be exceeded.

Section 2: Cargo Gear Inspection
Cargo gear must be inspected annually by a competent authority. Chief Mate must keep records of all cargo gear inspections and certificates in the Ship Safety log.

Section 3: Dangerous Cargo Regulations
Handling of dangerous goods must comply with local port regulations. Proper safety equipment must be worn.
No mention of MARPOL Annex III or SOLAS Chapter VII compliance certificates or STCW specialized training requirements.
Emergency response drills for chemical spills are conducted once every 12 months.
